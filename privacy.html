Privacy Policy for Windows 11 New Tab

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use the "Windows 11 New Tab" Chrome Extension (the "Extension").

1. Information We Collect

When you choose to log in using Google OAuth, the Extension requests access to basic profile information, specifically your email address and name.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information accessed via Google OAuth is used strictly for authentication purposes and to personalize your local experience within the Extension. We do not store, log, or transmit your personal data to any external servers.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
